Local Watermark Studio

Batch watermark images on your own computer. Files are processed by this app and are not sent to an external service.

1. Images

PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP and TIFF. Multiple files are packed into one ZIP download.
Use a transparent PNG or WebP logo for the cleanest result.
Use text, a logo, or both. Leave text blank to use only your logo.
Select images to see the batch summary. This page does not upload anything until you click Process.
Recommended: repeated watermark, 30% opacity, 5% text size, WebP quality 88, maximum 2400 px. Preview on a sample before processing your full catalogue.

2. Watermark settings

Higher opacity is more visible and generally harder to remove cleanly.
Percentage of the shorter image dimension.
Adds independent randomized RGB variation to watermark pixels only. It can make the mark less uniform, but does not reliably trick AI removers.

3. Honey-pot protection

Recommended on: a prominent corner decoy plus a separate, soft-edged low-opacity mark over the central subject. This is a layered deterrent, not a guarantee that an AI remover will miss the second mark.
Example: © OVEN FRESH · OFFICIAL. This decoy is deliberately more conspicuous.
Separate text placed over the center of the image. It defaults to the main watermark text.
Soft edges make this layer less like a clean, isolated sticker. Excessive blur can make the mark invisible or easier to reconstruct.

4. Image output

Default off. Whole-image grain affects the photo as well as the watermark and is not a reliable AI-removal defense.
Used for WebP/JPEG. Lower values reduce file size but introduce artifacts; they do not guarantee better protection.
